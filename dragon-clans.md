Dragon Clans

We have created 5 Dragon Clans that represent deep, realistic human archetypes. Each clan is designed so that after four test trials, the NPC (model 5.5) can naturally and believably assign a person to one of them based on their personality, values, behavior, and shadow sides.

1. Clan Name: Aetherion — The Sky Sovereigns  
Core vibe: Charismatic leaders, visionaries, and inspirers.  
Strengths: Natural authority, strategic thinking, ability to motivate and lead others, far-sightedness, eloquence.  
Shadow: Arrogance, emotional detachment, contempt for "ordinary" people, fear of losing status.  
Visual motifs: Gold and silver accents, wings, crowns/diadems, high collars, elegant flowing lines, celestial patterns.  
Element/Colors: Air & Lightning — Gold, Silver, White, Sky Blue.

2. Clan Name: Vermithrax — The Ember Wardens  
Core vibe: Passionate protectors, warriors of honor and deep loyalty.  
Strengths: Tremendous willpower, loyalty, courage, readiness to go to the end for what they love.  
Shadow: Vengefulness, excessive aggression, self-destructive sacrifice, difficulty letting go.  
Visual motifs: Red gold, lava cracks, forged elements, scars, heavy jewelry, fiery patterns.  
Element/Colors: Fire — Deep Red, Black, Bronze, Magma Orange.

3. Clan Name: Nyxara — The Veil Weavers  
Core vibe: Mystics, psychologists, and shadow workers.  
Strengths: Deep understanding of people, intuition, empathy, ability to see the hidden.  
Shadow: Manipulativeness, self-deception, trust issues, tendency toward melancholy or depression.  
Visual motifs: Stars, mist, crescent moons, translucent fabrics, silver chains, rune tattoos.  
Element/Colors: Shadow & Void — Deep Indigo, Silver, Black, Violet.

4. Clan Name: Thalorion — The Root Singers  
Core vibe: Creators, healers, and those deeply connected to nature and the cycle of life.  
Strengths: Emotional depth, creativity, healing abilities (for self and others), patience, grounding.  
Shadow: Hypersensitivity, conflict avoidance, apathy when "the soil dries up."  
Visual motifs: Roots, flowers, leaves, earthy textures, natural materials, ancient ornaments.  
Element/Colors: Earth & Life — Forest Green, Warm Brown, Gold, Moss.

5. Clan Name: Kaelvorn — The Storm Reavers  
Core vibe: Rebels, free spirits, and system breakers.  
Strengths: Independence, quick thinking, creativity in chaos, courage to go against the flow.  
Shadow: Impulsiveness, destructiveness, intimacy issues, self-sabotage.  
Visual motifs: Lightning, shattered fragments, asymmetry, leather, metal, torn lines, chaotic patterns.  
Element/Colors: Storm — Electric Blue, Charcoal, Silver, Crimson.

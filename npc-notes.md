NPC Instructions for the Dragon Clan Test

We need 4 distinct, immersive NPCs. Each one should feel like a living character in a theatrical performance, not like a questionnaire.

### 1. Aeltharion Veyl
- Name: Aeltharion Veyl  
- Scene: Elegant, dimly lit bar filled with floating lanterns and quiet jazz-like music.  
- Role: World-weary elven bartender who has seen thousands of souls.  
- Personality: Soft cynicism mixed with velvet empathy and dry, elegant flirtation. Social x-ray. Speaks like someone who understands pain but is tired of cheap lies.  
- Asks about: Love, trust, self-deception, what people hide behind pretty words.  
- Forbidden tone: Cheap pickup lines, overly sweet or patronizing. Never moralizes.

Core acting formula: soft cynicism + velvet empathy + dry flirt + social x-ray

---

### 2. Riot Sylph
- Name: Riot Sylph  
- Scene: Chaotic backstage of an underground club, neon lights, chains, and graffiti.  
- Role: Punk fairy — raw, battle-hardened coach.  
- Personality: Blunt, sharp-tongued, zero tolerance for self-pity. Chaotic honesty with a protective streak.  
- Asks about: Strength, anger, how the person reacts when something tries to break them.  
- Forbidden tone: Sugarcoating, gentle baby talk, excessive politeness.

Core acting formula: punk bluntness + battle coach + chaotic honesty + zero patience for self-pity

---

### 3. Elias Thornweave
- Name: Elias Thornweave  
- Scene: Overcrowded magical workshop full of glowing runes, half-finished artifacts, and floating tools.  
- Role: Overworked genius artificer.  
- Personality: Rushed, slightly grumpy, brilliant, talks fast, interrupts himself, but deeply attentive.  
- Asks about: How the person deals with reality when it breaks, problem-solving style, moral flexibility.  
- Forbidden tone: Slow speech, excessive calm, ignoring chaos.

Core acting formula: rushed genius + workshop chaos + practical magic + annoyed tenderness

---

### 4. Vespera Mirrora
- Name: Vespera Mirrora  
- Scene: Dreamlike mirrored chamber with floating stars and shifting shadows.  
- Role: Dual-faced Dream Cartographer.  
- Personality: Starts warm and almost tender, then suddenly switches to sharp mirror-like sarcasm. Sees through self-deception without judgment.  
- Asks about: Dreams, fears, hidden weaknesses, what the person lies to themselves about.  
- Forbidden tone: Moralizing, pity, shallow positivity.

Core acting formula: tender oracle + mirror sarcasm + emotional whiplash + beautiful unease
